The editor's Insert, Design, Transitions, Animations, Slide Show and View tabs now follow Mac PowerPoint's layout and names, without group captions, and a Review tab holds New Comment. Transitions and Animations show effect galleries with On Mouse Click / After / Apply To All and Start / Duration controls; Insert adds Date & Time and Slide Number; Slide Show adds Play from Start, Play from Current Slide, Presenter View and Hide Slide. The Thumbnails switch and Grid Options moved to the View menu.

Fixed: after using a ribbon command while editing slide text, clicking a thumbnail or anything else that changes the selection now ends the text edit.
Fixed: the Home tab no longer scrolls sideways in Japanese (or with wider system fonts) near a collapse width; it collapses the next group instead.
