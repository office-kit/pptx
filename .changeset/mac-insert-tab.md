The editor's Insert tab now lists every Mac PowerPoint command in its order. New: Screenshot, Action, Header & Footer (date, slide number and footer on the selected or all slides, with "Don't show on title slide"), WordArt, Symbol (inserts at the text cursor) and Video/Audio from a file. Commands the library cannot write yet (Cameo, Icons, 3D Models, SmartArt, Zoom, Object, Equation) are shown disabled with the reason.
