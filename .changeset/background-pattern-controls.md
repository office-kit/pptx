Edit slide background patterns with a pattern gallery and foreground/background theme color controls. Selected slides support undo, reset and saving. Editing an inherited pattern creates a slide override while preserving the layout or master and the original color transforms.

Background pattern readers accept `preserveTheme` to retain unmodified theme color references.
