The editor gains Draw and Record tabs. Draw has pen, eraser and Lasso Select tools, a pen gallery (black and red pens, pencil, highlighter) with Add Pen, Ink to Shape (lines, triangles, rectangles and ellipses) and Draw with Trackpad; strokes are saved as editable freeforms. Record plays the show from the beginning or the current slide.
