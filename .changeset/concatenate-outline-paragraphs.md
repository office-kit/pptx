Concatenate text from multiple shapes with `setShapeParagraphs(target, { sources })`, retaining paragraph formatting and hyperlinks. In Outline View, demote a slide title into the previous slide's body with Tab or the context menu, with Undo support. Slides with additional objects or no destination body placeholder report an error before changing their content.
