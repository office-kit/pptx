The preview no longer has a header bar: the editor fills the window. Agents opens from a new **Agents** button beside Comments and Share and docks as a task pane on the right, closed by default, in the editor's light or dark colors (including the terminal). **View ▸ Reading View** replaces the Preview button and opens the rendered viewer, whose status bar now holds Present, Presenter view, Download PPTX, Agents and **Normal** (Esc also returns to the editor). An agent's close button is now labelled "Close agent".
