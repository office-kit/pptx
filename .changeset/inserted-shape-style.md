Shapes drawn from the editor's Shapes gallery are visible again and match PowerPoint: they get the theme's "Colored Fill - Accent 1" style (accent fill, darker outline, light text), brackets and braces get the outline-only line style with dark text, text typed into them is centered both ways, and they are named like "Oval 3". Previously every inserted shape had neither fill nor outline, so only its selection box showed.
