The editor's Insert, Draw and Design tabs now match Mac PowerPoint's layout:

- Insert has PowerPoint's ▾ menus: Table offers a grid that inserts a table of the chosen size; Pictures, Screenshot, Video and Audio list PowerPoint's sources, with those the browser cannot reach shown disabled; Chart opens the chart dialog on the chosen type; Text Box ▾ can also draw a vertical text box. In a narrower window 3D Models, SmartArt and Chart become small rows and Date & Time, Slide Number and Object small icons, as in PowerPoint.
- Draw has Eraser ▾ (Stroke Eraser) and Add ▾ with Add Pen, Add Pencil and Add Highlighter.
- Design's Colors and Fonts menus list all of Office's color sets and font pairs, with the deck's current ones on top; Slide Size marks the current size; the Themes gallery fills the window's width. Office 2007 - 2010 fonts are now Calibri / Calibri, as PowerPoint lists them.
- Button sizes, group spacing and two-line captions follow PowerPoint in English and Japanese.
