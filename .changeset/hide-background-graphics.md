Add `isSlideBackgroundGraphicsHidden`, `setSlideBackgroundGraphicsHidden`, and `isSlideLayoutBackgroundGraphicsHidden` to inspect and control inherited decoration without deleting template content. Apply to All also copies the graphics visibility setting to slides and layouts. The preview honors both levels, and the editor's Format Background pane supports changing selected slides with undo and save/reload.
