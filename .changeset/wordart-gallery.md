The editor's WordArt gallery now matches PowerPoint's: Shape Format ▸ WordArt Quick Styles and Insert ▸ WordArt show all twenty presets as styled "A" swatches in PowerPoint's order, with the native names as tooltips. Picking one writes the same fill, outline, shadow, glow, reflection and pattern settings PowerPoint writes and replaces the previous WordArt style instead of layering on top of it. Theme-tinted colors stay linked to the deck's theme, and the Soft Bevel and Sharp Bevel presets write PowerPoint's text 3-D settings. Clear WordArt also removes a bevel.
