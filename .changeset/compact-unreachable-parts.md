`compactPackage` now removes every part that cannot be reached from the package root (`/_rels/.rels`) through internal relationships, not only `/ppt/media/` parts. `removeSlide` leaves the slide's charts, embedded workbooks, notes slide and tags in the package, so a deck that had slides removed or rebuilt kept growing and still carried the old chart data in the saved file; `compactPackage` now drops them, together with their `.rels` parts and `[Content_Types].xml` overrides. Parts that are still referenced — `presentation.xml`, document properties, themes, masters, every layout, the notes master and anything another slide shares — are kept. `getOrphanMediaPartNames` reports media by the same reachability, so media referenced only from a removed part (for example a chart's picture) now counts as orphaned. `compactPackage` throws when the package has no `/_rels/.rels` instead of removing every part; `getOrphanMediaPartNames` then reports every media part.
