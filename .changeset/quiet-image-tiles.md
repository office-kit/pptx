Render PNG and JPEG image fills as repeating tiles with alignment, offsets, independent scaling and alternating horizontal/vertical reflections. Use embedded PNG/JFIF resolution or an explicit fill DPI to size the tiles. Add `getShapeImageIntrinsicSize` to read the image's unscaled physical size.
